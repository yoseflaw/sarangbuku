# Sarang Buku

A free, private platform for communities to exchange physical books at mutual locations in Indonesia.

## Goal

Sharing is caring. Knowledge is power.

Our vision: no books should sit idly. In this platform, members maintain a bookshelf and wishlist, discover available books anonymously, and propose one-for-one permanent exchanges. Privacy is taken very seriously.

The MVP is successful when users can complete exchanges independently. The north-star metric is completed book swaps.

## MVP scope

- Invitation registration and public swap-zone selection
- Book catalog, owned physical copies, availability, and wishlists
- Anonymous discovery and search
- One-for-one exchange requests called Minat
- Accept, reject, and cancel flows without negotiation
- Post-acceptance coordination messages and email notifications
- Two-sided handover confirmation and atomic ownership transfer
- Problem reporting, swap history, and Django administration

No temporary swaps, sales, payments, delivery, ratings, public reputation, precise location tracking, real-time chat, or social feed.

## Stack

Django modular monolith, PostgreSQL, Django Templates, HTMX, Bootstrap, WhiteNoise, Django Admin, and Fly.io.
